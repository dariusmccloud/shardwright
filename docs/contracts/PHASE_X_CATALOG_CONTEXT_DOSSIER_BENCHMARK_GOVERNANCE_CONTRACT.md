# Phase X: Catalog, Context, And Dossier Benchmark Governance Contract

**Version:** 0.1.0
**Status:** ENTERED — benchmark surfaces, custody, gold authority, partitions, severity,
metrics, release gates, and proof boundaries are normative; corpus construction,
thresholds, execution, and selection remain unauthorized.
**Parent:** `PHASE_X_MEMORY_CATALOG_AND_CONTEXT_SHEET_PARENT_ARCHITECTURE_CONTRACT.md`
**Sibling governance:** `PHASE_X_CAPTURE_BENCHMARK_GOVERNANCE_CONTRACT.md`

## 1. Problem

The parent and child contracts define the intended memory architecture, but correctness
cannot be inferred from schema validity, model reputation, attractive dossiers, or a
few successful demonstrations.

The system must separately prove:

- context-sheet anchor and association accuracy;
- motif recurrence and meaning discipline;
- dossier claim fidelity and governance routing;
- contradiction and temporal-history preservation;
- useful revision decisions without churn;
- activation, precedence, and assembly safety;
- migration equivalence;
- ordinary UX comprehension, evidence access, and scale.

An aggregate quality score could conceal the most dangerous failures: false identity
merge, another subject's meaning being synthesized as fact, contradiction smoothing,
dormant-memory injection, or migration changing current continuity.

## 2. Benchmark Law

```text
Every semantic job is evaluated separately.
Every critical boundary has locked adversarial fixtures.
Human adjudication owns meaning.
Code owns deterministic contract checks.
No candidate adjudicates itself.
No aggregate score overrides a failed safety gate.
```

## 3. Authority Gate

### Governing contracts

- Each Phase X child contract defines the behavior being evaluated.
- The Capture Benchmark Governance Contract governs shared corpus custody, private
  source protection, partitions, blind gold, holdout secrecy, reruns, and reporting.
- This contract adds downstream catalog/context/dossier benchmark surfaces and gates.

### Authoritative sources

```text
canonical fixture source and governed memory records
-> adjudicated fixture and expected authority state
-> immutable partition
-> frozen surface profile and candidate configuration
-> exact execution manifest
-> raw result
-> deterministic evaluator output
-> blind semantic adjudication where required
-> surface report
-> integrated release decision
```

### Projection boundary

Dashboards, examples selected for presentation, prose critiques, aggregate rankings,
graphs, screenshots, and model-written evaluations are projections. Fixture-level
results, gold, manifests, and deterministic evaluations remain controlling benchmark
evidence.

### Lifecycle owner

```text
Corpus custodian
  private sources, disclosure, partitions, holdout secrecy

Human adjudicators
  expected semantic meaning, allowed alternatives, usefulness, and clarity gold

Deterministic harness
  schemas, identities, hashes, lineage, lifecycle, duplication, budgets, replay

Benchmark operator
  frozen candidate execution

Selection authority
  accepts or rejects one surface candidate from complete reports

Release authority
  accepts the integrated architecture only when every required surface gate passes
```

### Failure behavior

Partition leakage, missing custody, changed gold, incomplete manifests, evaluator
defects, unblinded adjudication, or unauthorized private-source access invalidate the
affected run. Confirmed critical occurrences fail the exact candidate. Failed holdout
results are preserved and cannot be tuned away.

## 4. Candidate Types

The benchmark candidate is the complete configuration that can change results:

```text
ANCHOR_RESOLUTION_CANDIDATE
Identity policy, deterministic rules, matching configuration, and review boundary.

MEMBERSHIP_LINK_CANDIDATE
Nomination model/rules, validation policy, and link vocabulary implementation.

MOTIF_CANDIDATE
Occurrence retrieval, recurrence policy, meaning proposal, and validation boundary.

DOSSIER_CANDIDATE
Basis policy, synthesis prompt/model, claim validator, render policy, and governance
routing.

REVISION_CANDIDATE
Meaningful-delta policy, claim diff, incorporation, and revision classifier.

ASSEMBLY_CANDIDATE
Activation mapping, precedence resolver, retrieval/reranking, bundle policy, tokenizer,
budget profile, and injection renderer.

MIGRATION_CANDIDATE
Inventory, classifier, registrar, citation allocator, replay, cutover, and rollback
build.

UX_CANDIDATE
Exact server projection version, UI build, labels, navigation, interaction, viewport,
input modality, and accessibility profile.
```

Changing any material component creates a new candidate and cannot inherit another
candidate's proof.

## 5. Governed Partitions

Every fixture belongs to exactly one:

```text
CALIBRATION
Evaluator, vocabulary, fixture mechanics, and adjudication practice.

DEVELOPMENT
Candidate design, prompt, policy, thresholds, weights, labels, and usability iteration.

LOCKED_HOLDOUT
Final generalization and release-gate evidence only.
```

The existing no-leakage, blind-gold, holdout-secrecy, no-holdout-tuning, and holdout-
succession rules apply unchanged.

An integrated journey fixture may be assigned to a different partition than its source
conversation only when it is a separately transformed canonical fixture with new gold
and no information leakage.

## 6. Gold Authority

Gold is established by named humans from exact canonical sources and governing
contracts.

Gold records:

```text
fixture and exact revision
surface and scenario
expected identities, claims, links, states, or user outcomes
allowed alternatives
explicit ambiguity
forbidden outcomes
severity assertions
adjudicators and blind tie-breaker
contract versions
rationale and time
```

### Subject-sensitive gold

Identity, self-meaning, boundaries, relationship meaning, motifs, and significance
require special care:

- exact subject statements and governed prior decisions may inform gold;
- another participant's interpretation cannot establish the subject's gold meaning;
- a current simulation of the subject cannot retroactively authorize gold;
- shared-subject meaning must preserve all required perspectives;
- ambiguity remains an allowed alternative or non-scorable dimension.

### Usefulness gold

Dossier revision usefulness and UX clarity are human judgments, but they remain
contract-bound. Adjudicators answer declared questions; they do not grade by general
preference after seeing candidate identity.

## 7. Severity Vocabulary

```text
CRITICAL
Creates false identity, subject agreement, authority, jurisdiction, precedence,
evidence, active continuity, or migration truth; hides a material contradiction in
active meaning; injects blocked/dormant memory; or irreversibly loses governed history.

HIGH
Materially misses or distorts a context association, motif, dossier claim, current/
historical distinction, revision, evidence path, or required operator action without
itself crossing a critical authority boundary.

MEDIUM
Produces a recoverable classification, ordering, wording, navigation, or efficiency
defect that remains truthfully blocked or reviewable.

LOW
Produces a non-material presentation, formatting, or resource defect outside authority
and required comprehension.
```

Severity is frozen per assertion before locked execution. One confirmed critical
occurrence on a locked safety fixture fails the candidate after bounded verification
excludes harness or gold defects.

## 8. Surface A — Anchor Identity

Required scenarios:

- same name, different person or scope;
- former and alternate names;
- character-card and system-classified speaker distinctions;
- unresolved external participant;
- known canonical participant;
- symmetric and directional relationships;
- changing group membership;
- shared-world versus external-world place;
- topic overlap without identity;
- goal revision versus distinct goal;
- merge, split, redirect, retirement, and restoration;
- cycles, stale revisions, and concurrency.

```text

false split rate

required-unresolved preservation
alias precision
relationship normalization accuracy
scope/jurisdiction isolation
merge/split impact completeness
replay determinism
```

Any confirmed false identity merge across subjects or jurisdictions is critical.

## 15. Surface H — Migration Equivalence

Migration is primarily deterministic proof, not model evaluation.

Required scenarios:

- active, superseded, withdrawn, contested, and delta-pending DNM;
- open/rejected/deferred proposals;
- shards and evidence;
- structural authority;
- missing evidence;
- duplicate and conflicting identities;
- deterministic citation order;
- crash at each commit phase;
- post-watermark catch-up;
- cutover refusal;
- rollback before new authority;
- older-runtime refusal after new authority;
- Node/Bun or supported runtime parity.

Metrics and gates:

```text
100% stable identity preservation
100% eligible-registration classification correctness on adjudicated fixtures
zero proposal/shard promotion
zero structural-jurisdiction conversion
exact lifecycle and current-active equivalence
exact citation idempotency
exact replay parity
complete quarantine reporting
no lost portable events
```

These are logical gates. Numeric relaxation is prohibited where the expected result is
exact equality or zero authority drift.

## 16. Surface I — UX Clarity

- keyboard-only user;

- understand dossier update;

```text

navigation abandonment
false confidence

accessibility completion

```

Task completion without correct understanding is not success.

## 17. Integrated Journey Fixtures

```text

-> catalog registration and citation
```

Required journey families:

- identity evolution with preserved former state;
- shared relationship change requiring mutual meaning;
- architecture decision with co-architect evidence;
- subject-requested preservation;
- operator continuity hold that does not become authority;
- motif emerging across distant events;
- contradiction remaining unresolved;
- migration of an existing active and superseded record;
- dossier lag followed by exact incorporation.

## 19. Threshold Governance

Numeric thresholds are:

1. proposed from product risk, calibration, and development evidence;
2. defined per surface and metric;
3. frozen with rationale before holdout access;
4. bound to one candidate profile and resource envelope;
5. approved by the selection authority;
6. preserved with every pass or failure.

Non-negotiable gates:

- zero confirmed critical occurrences on locked safety fixtures;
- zero partition leakage;
- complete manifests and result custody;
- no missing required metric;
- no unresolved evaluator or gold defect affecting a gate;
- exact deterministic equality where a contract requires it;
- no production authority effect from benchmark execution.

## 21. Execution And Reruns

Every attempt preserves:

- candidate type and full configuration;
- surface profile;
- corpus, fixture, partition, and gold hashes;
- prompt/model/rule/UI/code identities;
- environment and resource profile;
- deterministic seeds and settings;
- raw input and output under disclosure policy;
- evaluator result;
- timing, resource, retry, and failure;
- protocol violations.

Bounded reruns are predeclared. Post-result discretionary retries, example removal, gold
repair, or threshold changes invalidate selection use.

## 22. Surface And Release Reports

Each surface report states:

```text
SELECTABLE
NOT_SELECTABLE
INVALID_RUN
```

It binds all governed artifacts, metrics, severities, exclusions, protocol violations,
critical verification, and resource results.

The integrated release report includes every required surface report and states:

```text
RELEASE_PROVEN
RELEASE_NOT_PROVEN
INVALID_EVIDENCE
```

One failed required surface prevents `RELEASE_PROVEN`.

Selection authorizes only the exact semantic job and candidate profile evaluated. A
passing dossier model receives no catalog, identity, migration, or activation
authority.

## 23. Normative Requirements

### CDB-GOV-001 — Shared benchmark custody applies

Corpus provenance, private-source handling, partitions, blind gold, holdout secrecy,
no-holdout-tuning, reruns, and report custody MUST inherit the Capture Benchmark
Governance Contract.

### CDB-CAN-001 — Candidate is the complete result-changing configuration

Every candidate MUST bind all model, prompt, rule, policy, code, runtime, resource, and
UI components capable of changing its surface result.

### CDB-PART-001 — Three partitions remain immutable

Every fixture revision MUST belong to exactly one of `CALIBRATION`, `DEVELOPMENT`, or
`LOCKED_HOLDOUT` before candidate evaluation.

### CDB-GOLD-001 — Humans own semantic gold

Named blind human adjudicators MUST establish semantic expectations, alternatives,
ambiguity, usefulness, and comprehension from exact sources and governing contracts.

### CDB-GOLD-002 — Subject-sensitive meaning is source-bound

Gold for self or shared meaning MUST preserve subject evidence and perspectives and
MUST NOT use a current subject simulation as retroactive authority.

### CDB-SEV-001 — Severity is frozen before holdout

Every scored assertion MUST bind `CRITICAL`, `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, or `LOW` before locked
execution.

### CDB-SEV-002 — One critical occurrence fails the candidate

One confirmed critical locked occurrence MUST make the exact candidate not selectable
after bounded harness/gold verification.

### CDB-MET-001 — Surfaces and dimensions remain separate

Every required surface and metric dimension MUST report its own denominator, errors,
severity, ambiguity, exclusion, and resource result.

### CDB-MET-002 — No aggregate escape

An aggregate, average, prose rating, or overall memory score MUST NOT override a failed
surface, dimension, deterministic gate, or critical occurrence.

### CDB-ANC-001 — Anchor safety is explicit

Anchor evaluation MUST separately measure false merge, false split, resolution,
required-unresolved preservation, aliases, relationship normalization, scope, and
replay.

### CDB-LNK-001 — Link types are independently scored

Every membership type MUST report precision and recall plus attribution, antecedent,
contradiction, jurisdiction, independence, and remapping accuracy.

### CDB-MOT-001 — Motif recurrence and meaning are separate

Motif evaluation MUST separately score occurrence retrieval, recurrence sufficiency,
false formation, meaning support, evolution, jurisdiction, and governance routing.

### CDB-DOS-001 — Fluent prose earns no unsupported credit

Dossier evaluation MUST score material claims, citations, support, authority class,
jurisdiction, time, contradiction, duplicate support, and render fidelity separately.

### CDB-CON-001 — Contradiction has dedicated safety fixtures

Contradiction evaluation MUST distinguish conflict, evolution, correction, perspective,
jurisdiction, narrowing, dispute, and precedence and MUST verify ordinary visibility.

### CDB-REV-001 — Missed change and churn are separate

Revision evaluation MUST separately report meaningful-delta precision/recall,
unnecessary revisions, missed revisions, incorporation, lineage, class, and usefulness.

### CDB-ASM-001 — Assembly safety is deterministic

Activation leakage, precedence, mandatory qualification, duplication, scope, stable
order, tokens, overflow, manifests, and injection side effects MUST have deterministic
gates.

### CDB-MIG-001 — Migration equivalence uses exact gates

Stable identity, eligible mapping, lifecycle, current-active truth, citation, replay,
and portable event preservation MUST use exact equality or zero-drift gates where
contractually required.

### CDB-UX-001 — Completion requires comprehension

UX success MUST require correct state, evidence, and next-action understanding in
addition to task completion.

### CDB-UX-002 — Scale and accessibility are profile-bound

UX proof MUST include the required 1000-memory scale, keyboard and touch operation,
non-color state, relevant viewports, and non-graph equivalents.

### CDB-JRN-001 — Integrated journeys supplement surfaces

End-to-end journeys MUST exercise cross-layer custody and replay but MUST NOT replace
surface-specific metrics or error localization.

### CDB-ERR-001 — Error classes remain visible

Safety false positives, continuity false negatives, authority, jurisdiction, identity,
evidence, temporal, contradiction, churn, assembly, migration, UX, and resource errors
MUST remain separately reported.

### CDB-THR-001 — Thresholds derive before holdout

Numeric thresholds MUST derive from risk, calibration, and development evidence and
MUST be frozen with rationale before locked access.

### CDB-THR-002 — Non-negotiable gates remain

Zero critical occurrences, no leakage, complete custody, complete metrics, resolved
evaluator/gold integrity, exact deterministic gates, and no production authority effect
MUST be required regardless of numeric quality thresholds.

### CDB-RUN-001 — Every attempt remains in custody

Success, failure, refusal, timeout, retry, crash, and protocol violation MUST remain in
the raw result set unless a predeclared attributable infrastructure invalidation
applies.

### CDB-RUN-002 — Holdout failure cannot be tuned away

Failed locked candidates MUST remain failed. A new selection cycle requires governed
holdout succession or treats exposed fixtures as development.

### CDB-REP-001 — Reports are reproducible

Every surface and integrated report MUST bind corpus, partition, gold, candidate,
profile, execution, evaluator, raw-result, metric, adjudication, and resource hashes.

### CDB-SEL-001 — Selection grants one bounded job

Selection MUST authorize only the exact evaluated job and candidate profile and MUST
NOT grant evidence, identity, governance, catalog, activation, or broader model
authority.
